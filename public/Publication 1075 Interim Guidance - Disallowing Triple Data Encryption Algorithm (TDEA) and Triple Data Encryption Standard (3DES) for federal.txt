SECURITY AND PRIVACY ALERT
Office of Safeguards
 
Date: February 6, 2024
 
Subject: Publication 1075 Interim Guidance - Disallowing Triple Data Encryption Algorithm (TDEA) / Triple Data Encryption Standard (3DES) for federal data
 
Announcement
As of January 1, 2024, the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) has withdrawn Special Publication (SP) 800-67 Revision 2, Recommendation for the Triple Data Encryption Algorithm (TDEA) Block Cipher. TDEA/3DES is no longer a supported algorithm to protect federal data which includes federal tax information (FTI).
 
Guidance
Agencies must immediately stop the use of TDEA/3DES to protect FTI. 
Per NIST guidance, agencies should use Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) instead of TDEA/3DES.
Agencies may continue to use TDEA/3DES for the decryption, key unwrapping and verification of message authentication codes (MAC) s of already protected data.
 
Additional information
NIST SP 800-131A Revision 2, Transitioning the Use of Cryptographic Algorithms and Key Lengths further explains the acceptability of algorithms used for cryptographic protection of federal data.
